## Interpretation

Fusion removes off-chip materializations but reserves two streaming slots and can add on-chip routes. Split regions drain through DRAM before the next region starts. The heuristic uses aggregate resource load plus fill/drain cost; simulation additionally enforces dependencies, buffer-slot reuse and simultaneous resource reservations.

Always-split and always-fuse select tiles and placements by the same analytical estimate. Always-fuse is reported as illegal when its subset has no legal candidate. The second matmul, when requested, is a separate materialized region in every policy.
